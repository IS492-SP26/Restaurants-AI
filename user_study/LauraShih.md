# User Study Feedback Summary

## Overview

This document summarizes qualitative feedback from two participants who tested the Multi-Agent Restaurant Launch Planning Tool. Participants were asked to use the tool and answer post-task interview questions about usefulness, clarity, trust, local grounding, and improvement opportunities.

# Participant 1: Viral

## Raw Feedback

1. The option to select ideas is really helpful because it helps with clarification.
2. The startup budget range on the “Basics” page is preselected to “$75K–$200K.” This can be improved by letting the user select the budget directly.
3. The AI suggested business-proof ideas while considering the geospatial economy.
4. The manual is beginner-friendly and easy to navigate.
5. For the “Legal / Licensing Checklist,” the participant liked that the AI clearly indicates the content is educational and not legal advice. However, the tool could be improved by redirecting users to official government websites where the actual laws and requirements are listed.
6. The participant would trust this tool for early founders because it provides robust planning for starting a business.

## Key Takeaways

### Positive Feedback

- The idea selection feature helps users clarify their restaurant direction.
- The AI-generated suggestions felt business-oriented and locally aware.
- The manual was easy to navigate and beginner-friendly.
- The legal disclaimer was helpful and increased trust.
- The tool was seen as useful for early-stage founders.

### Improvement Suggestions

- Allow users to directly select or customize the startup budget range.
- Add links to official government websites for legal and licensing information.

# Cross-Participant Summary

## Positive Themes

### 1. The tool helps users start from a vague idea

Both participants found the tool useful for early-stage brainstorming. The system helps users move from a broad restaurant idea to a more structured plan.

### 2. Budget and planning breakdowns are useful

Both participants mentioned that the financial or planning structure helped make the business idea clearer.

### 3. The manual is beginner-friendly but information-heavy

Participants generally found the manual understandable and organized. However, one participant noted that there is a lot of information at once, so the manual may need better progressive disclosure or summarization.

### 4. Trust is improved by transparency

The legal disclaimer was positively received because it made clear that the tool provides educational guidance rather than official legal advice.

---

# Common Improvement Areas

## 1. More flexible budget input

The current preselected budget range may limit the user. Users should be able to directly select or customize their budget.

## 2. More specific and realistic data

Participants wanted more concrete values, assumptions, and locally grounded evidence behind the AI suggestions.

## 3. Stronger source transparency

For legal, licensing, and regulatory information, the tool should link to official government sources.

## 4. Reduce information overload

The generated manual is useful, but the system could make it easier to digest by adding summaries, collapsible sections, or step-by-step guidance.

---

# Preliminary Interpretation

The early user feedback suggests that the Multi-Agent Restaurant Launch Planning Tool is useful as a brainstorming and early-stage planning assistant. Participants found the concept selection, budget breakdown, and structured manual helpful for reducing the uncertainty of starting a restaurant.

However, the feedback also shows that users need stronger transparency before fully trusting the tool for real business decisions. In particular, users want clearer data sources, more realistic financial assumptions, and links to official legal or licensing information.

Overall, the tool appears promising for helping beginner founders organize their thinking, but future iterations should focus on improving local data grounding, source citation, and output readability.
