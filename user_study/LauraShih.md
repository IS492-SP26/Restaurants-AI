# Raw User Feedback

## Overview

This document records raw feedback from participant testing of the Multi-Agent Restaurant Launch Planning Tool.

Participants are anonymized as P1 and P2 to protect privacy.

The evaluation includes two types of feedback:

1. **Open-ended interview questions**  
   These questions do not have numerical scores. They are used to collect participants’ written comments and reactions.

2. **System Usability Scale (SUS) survey questions**  
   These questions use a 1–5 rating scale.

---

## Score Scale for SUS Survey

For the SUS survey questions, participants rated each statement on a 1–5 Likert scale:

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Strongly disagree |
| 2 | Disagree |
| 3 | Neutral |
| 4 | Agree |
| 5 | Strongly agree |

Note: The open-ended interview questions do not have scores.
