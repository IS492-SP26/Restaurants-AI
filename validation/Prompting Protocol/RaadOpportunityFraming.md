### Key Validation Insights (Raad)

Based on interviews with local owners and operators, our tool addresses four critical operational gaps:

* **Dynamic Prep:** Replaces static plans with real-time adjustments based on Green Street weather and foot traffic to prevent food waste.
* **Student Labor Friction:** Integrates UIUC academic calendars to automate staffing schedules around midterms and seasonal breaks.
* **Menu Bloat:** Acts as a "Mean Uncle" critic to identify low-margin items and high-waste ingredients that students aren't actually buying.
* **Compliance Gaps:** Digitizes manual health code binders into automated, sensor-driven alerts to prevent business-ending safety errors.
