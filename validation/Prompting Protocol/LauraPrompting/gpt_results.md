# GPT Results

## Prompt 1 — Market Feasibility

### Summary
GPT gave a clear and structured answer with sections for market demand, competitors, and feasibility. It recognized that Green Street is student-heavy and that Chinese food demand is likely strong. It also identified competition as a major challenge.

### Strengths
- clear structure
- readable explanation
- useful high-level feasibility reasoning
- recognizes need for differentiation

### Weaknesses
- likely mixes reliable local knowledge with uncertain restaurant names/details
- some competitor references may be hallucinated or outdated
- reasoning is still somewhat generic despite mentioning Green Street
- no clear source transparency

### Key Observation
GPT sounds confident, which makes the answer feel useful, but this could also create trust issues if the local competitor list is inaccurate.

## Prompt 2 — Startup Cost Estimation

### Summary
GPT provided a straightforward startup cost table including rent, kitchen equipment, renovation, staff hiring, licenses, inventory, POS, and marketing. The answer was easy to read and easy for a first-time founder to understand.

### Strengths
- includes major startup categories
- provides a helpful range instead of a single number
- practical note that \$120,000 is possible but tight
- good beginner-friendly explanation

### Weaknesses
- cost ranges are broad and not clearly grounded in local evidence
- some assumptions may be generic to U.S. restaurants rather than Green Street specifically
- no explanation of how the numbers were derived
- may give a false sense of precision

### Key Observation
GPT is strong at producing a clean summary, but weaker at showing why its numbers should be trusted.

## Prompt 4 — Budget Constraint Scenario

### Summary
GPT adapted well to the lower budget and recommended a takeout-first model, smaller footprint, narrower menu, lean staffing, and used equipment. It also prioritized actions in a useful way.

### Strengths
- practical and clear prioritization
- realistic pivot from dine-in to lean format
- strong explanation of tradeoffs
- useful for early decision making

### Weaknesses
- still not strongly locally grounded
- no explicit discussion of feasibility risks if no second-generation space is found
- recommendations remain conceptual rather than tied to real local options

### Key Observation
This was one of GPT’s stronger outputs because the task rewarded general strategic reasoning more than local accuracy.

## Prompt 5 — Regulatory Guidance

### Summary
GPT provided a structured overview of business registration, health permits, food safety certifications, inspections, and city-related approvals. It also described the general order in which founders should complete these tasks.

### Strengths
- well-organized
- covers multiple levels of regulation
- useful sequence of steps
- identifies important local actors like CUPHD and City of Champaign

### Weaknesses
- still needs fact-checking because permit guidance is high-risk
- may mix general restaurant licensing knowledge with local requirements
- no citations or links for verification
- founders could over-trust the answer

### Key Observation
GPT is helpful for orientation, but not reliable enough to be used alone for legal or regulatory decisions.
