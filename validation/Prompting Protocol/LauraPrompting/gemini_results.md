## Prompt 1 — Market Feasibility

### Summary
Gemini gave a strong, business-style answer emphasizing Green Street as a high-traffic UIUC corridor. It framed the area as highly saturated and highlighted demographic alignment between Chinese cuisine and UIUC’s student population.

### Strengths
- strong market framing
- useful language around saturation and competition
- practical idea of second-generation space
- clearer business positioning than GPT

### Weaknesses

- sounds highly confident even when local evidence is uncertain

### Strengths
- strong comparison structure
