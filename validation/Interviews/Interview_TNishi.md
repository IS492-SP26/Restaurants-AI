Interview 1
Interviewee: Vice President of a company operating Izakaya (Japanese pubs) in Japan

Question and Answers Translated in English:
**1. Currently, if a person were to set up a food business from scratch, what sort of information would they struggle to acquire? Or often underestimate?**

Some of the most important an owner would miss or not consider is the local traffic situation. More specifically, a key metric they should consider are local public transportation schedule, including when is the last train or bus/

**2. There are certainly examples where a shop can fail despite its services and quality of food being good. What are some of the common causes for this?**

Even if the services an owner or a store can provide is really good, if they can’t understand the local consumer preferences or potential needs - then they can fail.

**3. In the food business, are there any tasks you believe can handle better than a human worker? Additionally, do you have any reservation about certain tasks being handled by AI?**

For tasks that I know that AI could handle well are managing inventory efficiently. AI can automate the process of ordering new food or items based on the revenue performance of the store. But, an operation that I don’t want AI to handle for now are dealing with clients. This includes dealing with customers or potential partners.

**4. Assuming a founder is successful in launching a store, how can Generative AI and AI tools help further expand the business?**

This really depends on where the founder wants to expand their business. Generally, though, market intelligence and surveys of potential location would be useful. 

**5. What do you think are the greatest challenges a new owner or existing owners face in the current food industry?**

There are lot of challenges the can force. This includes shortage of labor, training the staff, and especially cost control. In the food industry, cost is dominated by what is called the “FL Cost” (Food and Labor). As the price of ingredients and staff takes up a lot of the revenue, you need to have a clear business plan. Also depending on the location, the rent cost of the store can go really high if it sits on a really favorable area. Ideally you want the total cost of FLR cost (R for Rent) to be with 65% of the Revenue. 

**6. Based on the 3 principles AI agent we have (marketing, finance, legal expert), do you believe these are enough to cover the opening or continued operation of a store? (Do you have any additional advice to improve the framework)**

When it comes to simple research AI definitely does the job. But I think its also still important for owners to actually physically to the location they want to set up shop. Check their competitors, opening times, foot traffic. Based on that you need to analyze the market size, value, customer needs, and position, and come up with a detailed business plan. 

When thinking about getting capital form finance insitutions, you need a business plan backed up by evidence. Also think about the place in terms of getting labor and staff. For example, does your closest station connected to any universities or trade schools.
